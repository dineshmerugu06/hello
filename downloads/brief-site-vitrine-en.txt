PROFILE BRIEF — YOUR JOB SEARCH
A scoping document to complete with your agent; it is the single source of truth for every resume. It does not replace the agreed scope.

1. Your search in a few choices

Brief name
To clarify — A title is enough. No personal name or email needed.

First search or a switch?
To clarify — First search; or current approach, reason for change and what to keep.

Experience and skills
To clarify — Roles held, limits, target locations and public contact details to display.

Who should hire you, and their questions
To clarify — Who reads your resume? What do they need to know before contacting you?

Career goal
To clarify — What should change for your career; say if you lack reliable data.

Employer action and handling replies
To clarify — Email, call or interview; who receives replies and who answers?

Sections and information needed
To clarify — For each section: the employer question it answers.

Features, tools and priorities
To clarify — Expected use and connected tool; essential, handled separately or later.

2. What you already have

Resume and career information
To clarify — Available / to produce; who supplies facts, writes, approves and when?

Certificates, portfolio and format identity
To clarify — Available files and rights; creation to arrange; approval owner.

Usable evidence
To clarify — Qualifications, work or real references; permissions needed; otherwise, what is missing.

Personal information and data
To clarify — Facts to provide or verify; form data, recipients, retention and third-party tools.

3. Organising the search

Initial and ongoing budget
To clarify — Budgets, currency, period; drafting, documents, third-party services and changes to include or arrange.

Schedule and approvals
To clarify — Desired or fixed date, approver, availability and known dependencies.

Accounts, services and access needed
To clarify — Holder, manager and accounts involved, email to preserve. No passwords.

Application acceptance criteria
To clarify — Mobile journey, test application tracked, accurate information, specific checks to agree.

After applying
To clarify — Who renews, maintains and updates? Who reviews replies and outcomes?

Agent response requested: deliverables, exclusions, drafting work, included revisions, conditional schedule and ongoing costs. Confirm all unresolved points.
