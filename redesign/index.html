Questions, answered straight

Twelve of the twenty-three questions Imperium answers. The full set ships with the app.

What is Imperium?

Imperium is a local, career-focused personal agent. A Commander takes your plain-words command and delegates to specialists: a Job Agent that finds real jobs, a Resume Studio that tailors your resume, a Research Agent that studies companies, an Automation Agent that fills forms, and a Tracker that follows every application. It runs on your own machine, with a local Ollama brain and a local SQLite database. No cloud, no subscription, no API keys.

Does Imperium ever submit for me?

No — never. There is no submit function anywhere in Imperium, not even a disabled one. The agent fills the form, shows the checklist, and stops. Only you click Submit, and the application becomes “submitted” in the Tracker only when you confirm it with proof.

Why run a job agent locally instead of a cloud service?

With Imperium, your profile, resume and job history never leave your machine, so there is nothing to leak, sell or train on. A cloud service needs your data on its servers and a subscription to pay for them; Imperium needs neither. The work continues after each application too: the Tracker watches every application and warns you when one goes stale, so your search remains useful instead of being launched and left untouched.

How much does Imperium cost?

Imperium is free: every mode costs $0, because the software, the local Ollama brain and the database all run on your own machine. There are no subscriptions, setup fees or API keys. Nothing is taken when you sign up, because there is no payment at all. Every capability is included in every mode. Imperium charges no VAT and no fees, because nothing is sold: it is your software, on your computer.

What’s the difference between Local Logic, Ollama 3B and Ollama 7B?

Every mode includes the same agents, the same database and the same locked flow. Only the brain changes. Local Logic, $0 runs Imperium on deterministic code alone: filters, scoring and tracking, with no AI model, and It always works, even with Ollama off. Ollama 3B, $0 adds a light local model for drafting and summaries, with faster answers on modest hardware. Ollama 7B, $0, includes everything in Ollama 3B, with the strongest local reasoning (qwen2.5:7b), plus deep resume tailoring, guardrail fact-checking and full delegation.

Can Imperium use my existing resume?

Yes. Imperium builds your profile from your existing resume, and your profile becomes the single source of truth. Every tailored resume, form fill and summary is checked against it, and anything not in your profile is never invented.

Where do the jobs come from?

Imperium fetches real, live listings from public job sources: Remotive, Arbeitnow and Jobicy. Every source is named in the app, and every search shows a filter report: how many jobs came in, how many each filter removed, and why. Listings are deduplicated by company, title and URL, and salary and location are normalised so similar jobs can be compared honestly. Scores are computed by deterministic code against your profile — matched skills, missing skills, title and location — never invented by the language model.

Will my resume be optimized for ATS?

Every mode includes ATS scoring against the exact job description: matched keywords, missing keywords and a plain-language explanation, re-scored after every edit. Resumes are written only from your profile, so every keyword claimed is one you can defend in an interview. The aim is a resume an applicant tracking system can read and a recruiter can trust. Imperium does not guarantee a particular ATS score, an interview or a job offer.

What’s included in Research Notes?

For any saved job, the Research Agent gathers public information about the company and the role — what it does, its size and location if publicly known, its stated tech stack and recent public signals — and stores it as a sourced note attached to the job. Like everything in Imperium, it costs $0 and is included in every mode, with no maximum of notes per job. Every claim carries its source; anything without a source is labelled an inference or left out.

How much does Form Filling cost?

Form Filling costs $0, like everything else. The Automation Agent maps every form field to your profile or resume, fills what it can, blocks what it must — CAPTCHA, password, OTP and unknown fields — and stops before Submit.

What happens if a job closes before I apply?

Jobs close, and Imperium says so honestly. If a listing disappears, the job is marked closed with its real date and your saved search simply moves on to the next matching listing. Freshness is computed from the real posted date, and stale applications are flagged after 7 days with no real change, so nothing quietly rots in your tracker. No status is ever invented to make the pipeline look busy.

Can I stop using Imperium at any time?

Yes. There is no subscription to cancel. You simply stop running Imperium, or delete it, whenever you like. Your applications, resumes and research stay in your local database, on your machine, whether Imperium is running or not. Nothing is held on a server, so nothing needs to be cancelled, refunded or exported before a deadline. Deleting the local database deletes your data completely.
